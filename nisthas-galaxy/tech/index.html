room 01 / privacy

Nothing leaves the device.

Wick runs entirely on your phone’s own chip. No server, no account, no upload. Ask it about your medical results or your bank statement and the question never touches a network.

room 02 / size

Forty megabytes.

Smaller than the photos from your last holiday. Wick answers in under eighty milliseconds and works the same in a tunnel, on a plane, or in a village with one bar of signal.

room 03 / how it fits

Three steps to small.

  1. 01 distilA large teacher model trains a small student on exactly the tasks people use a phone assistant for.
  2. 02 quantiseWeights drop to four bits, with the layers that matter most kept wider.
  3. 03 shipThe model compiles for each phone’s neural engine, so it runs where the chip is fastest.
